Gaussian Blur – smooth, natural blur, heavier on performance.

Box Blur – cheaper, more blocky, useful for real-time.

Directional Blur / Motion Blur – blurs along a direction (simulating speed).

Radial Blur – blur radiating from a center point.

Bokeh Blur – simulates out-of-focus camera lens shapes.

Zoom Blur – makes it look like the scene is zooming into/out of a point.

Lens Distortion / Barrel Distortion – simulates fisheye or wide-angle lens.

Heat Haze / Wave Distortion – wavy distortions like mirage heat shimmer.

🔹 Color & Stylization

Color Grading / LUTs – film-like looks via color remapping.

Sepia / Grayscale / Duotone – simple aesthetic looks.

Posterization – reduces number of colors for a stylized effect.

Edge Detection (Sobel, Laplacian) – outlines objects like a sketch.

Cartoon / Toon Shader – flat shading with outlines.

Pixelation / Mosaic – “low-res” or retro game look.

Glitch / Chromatic Aberration – RGB channels split/offset for digital glitch look.

Scanlines / VHS Noise – retro CRT or VHS style.

🔹 Lighting & Depth-Like Effects

Bloom / Glow – bright areas bleed light.

HDR Tone Mapping – simulate filmic response.

Vignette – darkens edges of frame.

God Rays (Crepuscular Rays) – light scattering through bright spots.

Depth of Field (if depth info available) – selective blur on distance.

Refraction / Glass Shader – bend video as if through glass/water.

🔹 Fun / Abstract FX

Kaleidoscope / Mirror – symmetrical reflections.

Twirl / Swirl Distortion – rotates pixels around a point.

Ripple / Water Waves – circular sine distortions.

Displacement Mapping – distort video using another texture (e.g. noise).

Time-based Feedback (Echo) – delayed ghost images of previous frames.

Pixel Sorting – reorder pixels for glitch art style.
